# Business Case Study — Email Workflow Automation

## Business challenge

Recurring email requests require classification and contextual responses.

## Solution

Make, Gmail, Gemini and Google Drive support conditional routing, document grounding and human escalation.

## Competencies demonstrated

Process automation, prompt engineering, workflow architecture and governance.

## Documented evidence

Repository documents the architecture, functional flow and security limitations of the prototype.

## Scope and limitations

No importable blueprint or standalone executable; no validated time savings, accuracy or financial return.
